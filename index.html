Cécile Carrel, researcher in the geopolitics of AI

Cécile Carrel is a researcher working on the geopolitics of AI, with a focus on compute, the governance of AI models and Europe’s strategic dependencies.

She currently works at Augur AI, a research consultancy based in Washington DC, where she studies the positions of France and Sweden in the AI supply chain, from defence and sovereignty to opportunities for business, investment and policy. Her work draws on trade and export data, and on the newer instruments of geoeconomics that states use to advance their interests.

She began her career in technology at Amazon, as a transportation network engineer and in process engineering, before moving into statistical and analytical work at the World Economic Forum, the International Crisis Group and UNICEF. She has also worked with Meta and its Oversight Board on AI governance, national security and human rights.

She holds a Master of Public Policy from the London School of Economics, with Distinction, a bachelor’s in business with technology, informatics and computer science from emlyon business school, and a bachelor’s in sociology from the Sorbonne, where she trained in statistical analysis.

French and Thai, she was born near Geneva and grew up in New York, Switzerland and Hong Kong. She studied in Paris, Lyon and Mannheim, has worked in Berlin, Brussels, Geneva, Hamburg and Paris, and is based in London, with strong ties to Marseille and Bangkok.
