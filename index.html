Speaker bio

Cécile Carrel researches the geopolitics of AI, with a focus on compute, open-weight model governance and Europe’s strategic dependencies. She holds a Master of Public Policy from the London School of Economics, with Distinction, and has worked at the International Crisis Group, the World Economic Forum and UNICEF. She is based in London.
