Selected Research

Geopolitics & technology policy

  • Compute Governance & Strategic Dependencies (LSE, 2025–26). Europe's dependencies in AI compute: semiconductors, export control regimes and the geopolitics of AI capability. Develops an interdependence framework for the EU to strengthen deterrence and coordinate on compute governance with ASEAN, within US–China rivalry.
  • Technology, Conflict & ASEAN Diplomacy (LSE, 2025–26). Research on autonomous weapons systems, ASEAN non-interference norms and technology's role in the China–Taiwan security dynamic.
  • Building Durable AI Capability in UK Government (LSE, 2025). A transformation design brief on why the UK government has struggled to build institutional AI expertise, and a capability model to address it.

Human rights & AI governance

  • Human rights and the governance of generative AI (LSE, commissioned research, 2025–26). Research on how rules for dangerous organisations and violent extremist content hold up when generative AI and open-weight models change who produces and distributes content. The analysis is anchored in human rights law: the ICCPR's three-part test of legality, legitimate aim, and necessity and proportionality, and the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights. It centres on freedom of expression, counter-terrorism and national security content, and maps regulatory pathways in the EU and UK.

UNICEF

  • Maternal health dashboard for the Government of Eswatini (UNICEF, 2025, Data Lead). Translated the nationwide Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey (MICS) maternal health data into insights for the Eswatini government, identifying high-risk regions through time-series analysis. Built an interactive dashboard and a geospatial mapping tool across all survey indicators.

World Economic Forum

  • Engagement analytics for the Managing Board (World Economic Forum, 2023–24, Metrics, Data and Insights Specialist). Built the statistical foundation for the Forum's engagement analytics, covering its full portfolio across government, civil society and private-sector partners, and reported monthly insights to the Managing Board.

Chatham House

  • Digital Identity Policy Framework (Chatham House, Sep 2025). A policy framework for a UK government-backed digital identity system, covering governance architecture, interoperability standards and civil liberties safeguards. Recommendations were presented to experts from the Alan Turing Institute, the Tony Blair Institute, Chatham House and Yoti.

Amazon

  • Machine learning routing (Amazon, Spain). Deployed machine learning routing across Spain's air, ground and maritime network: delivery speed up 25%, ETA accuracy up 5%. Added fairness constraints to the linear programming objective, cutting manual overrides by 70%, and surfaced disparities across 10 fulfilment centres that triggered a Europe-wide fairness audit.
  • Robotics automation (Amazon, Hamburg). Robotics-assisted automation workflows in Python and Matlab: labour cycle time down 20%, forecast accuracy up 75%, scaled across 40 European sites for $2.4M in annual labour savings.
