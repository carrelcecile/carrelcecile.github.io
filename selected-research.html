Human rights & AI governance

How human rights law constrains the rules governing AI and harmful content.

LSE · Commissioned research 2025–26

Human rights and the governance of generative AI

Research on how rules for dangerous organisations and violent extremist content hold up when generative AI and open-weight models change who produces and distributes content. The analysis is anchored in human rights law: the ICCPR's three-part test of legality, legitimate aim, and necessity and proportionality, and the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights. It centres on freedom of expression, counter-terrorism and national security content, and maps regulatory pathways in the EU and UK.
