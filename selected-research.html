Human rights and AI governance

  • Human rights and the governance of generative AI (Meta Oversight Board, in collaboration with LSE, 2025–26)Co-authored commissioned research on how Meta’s policies on Dangerous Organizations and Individuals, national security, terrorism and disinformation hold up when generative AI and open-weight models change who produces content. It applies the ICCPR three-part test and the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, and maps regulatory pathways in the EU and the UK.
